## 📝 PART 1 — Interview (3 Questions)

### ❓ Q1: Do you prefer working alone or as part of a team? Why?

### 🗣️ Your base answer
> "I would like to do any project with a team because I want to build big things. So that there are a team that we understand and help each others what make me go further in the future."

### ✅ Strengths (IELTS Evaluation)
- Clear preference (team) + a strong reason (building big things) — good structure for Part 1.
- Shows ambition and collaboration mindset.

### 🔧 Corrections & Vocabulary Learned
| Original | Fixed |
|----------|-------|
| `I would like to do any project` | **I prefer working on projects** ("any" sounds odd/negative here) |
| `each others` | **each other** (always singular) |
| `..team that we understand... what make me...` | split into two clear sentences |
| `make me go further` | **pushes me to go further** |

### 💡 Fixed Answer (Band 7.0+)
> *"I prefer working on projects in a team, because I want to build big things. When team members understand and help each other, it pushes me to go further in my career."*

## ❓ Q2: Have you ever worked on a project with a tight deadline?

### 🗣️ Your base answer
> "I used to do a very close-deadline project with my partner. It was about 2 weeks before the deadline. Using ANN to mix it with classic AI algorithms, that was not easy game. Because my partner was not good at AI so she helped me to write down the document. Although, it was so close but we had finished the project before the deadline came. Then the overall core that we receiving was 9.0. Best ending for us."

### ✅ Strengths (IELTS Evaluation)
- Excellent storytelling — concrete project (**Snake_ANN**), timeline, roles, and result (9.0).
- Clear chronological flow — exactly what examiner wants in a narrative.

### 🔧 Corrections & Vocabulary Learned
| Original | Fixed |
|----------|-------|
| `I used to do a very close-deadline project` | **I once worked on a project with a very tight deadline** ("used to" = past habit, not one event) |
| `close-deadline` | **tight deadline** (standard IELTS collocation) |
| `Using ANN to mix it with classic AI algorithms` | **We combined an ANN with classical AI algorithms** |
| `that was not easy game` | **that was no easy game** (idiom) |
| `the overall core that we receiving` | **the overall score we received** (core ≠ score) |
| `it was so close but we had finished` | **it was extremely tight, but we managed to finish** |

### 💡 Fixed Answer (Band 7.0+)
> *"I once worked on a project with a very tight deadline with my partner. We had only two weeks left. We combined an Artificial Neural Network with classical AI algorithms — that was no easy game. Since my partner was not very strong in AI, she focused on writing the documentation. Although it was extremely tight, we managed to finish before the deadline, and the overall score we received was 9.0. It was the best possible ending for us."*

## ❓ Q3: What do you think makes someone an effective leader?

### 🗣️ Your base answer
> "After though out the window 3 fail projects, we got the highest score of the class. And we just done the most important tasks first, untill all the task was completed. In my opinion, to be an effective leader we have to understand listen the members of team. Because of supporting their problems is the assistance of the leader."

### ✅ Strengths (IELTS Evaluation)
- The core idea is correct and relevant: **listen + support team members**.
- Bonus: the "most important tasks first" idea shows real prioritization thinking.

### ⚠️ Critical IELTS issue
The first half ("3 failed projects → highest score of the class") answers a *different question* — it belongs to Q2's story. In Part 1, answer the question directly: **Direct answer → Reason → Example**.

### 🔧 Corrections & Vocabulary Learned
| Original | Fixed |
|----------|-------|
| `we have to understand listen the members` | **need to understand and listen to the members of the team** |
| `Because of supporting their problems is the assistance` | **Supporting them with their problems is a leader's main responsibility** |
| `we just done the most important tasks first` | **we prioritized the most important tasks first** |
| `untill all the task was completed` | **until all tasks were completed** |

### 💡 Fixed Answer (Band 7.0+)
> *"In my opinion, an effective leader needs to understand and listen to team members. A leader's main job is to support them in solving their problems. For example, in my project group, we prioritized the most important tasks first and managed to complete everything before the deadline."*

## 📝 PART 2 — Cue Card (Long Turn)

> **Describe a time you worked with a group on a project.**
> You should say:
> - what the project was and what role you played
> - who you worked with
> - how your team made decisions
> - and explain whether the collaboration was successful.

### 🗣️ Your base answer
> "At the time before, My teams with 2 members received requirement to create a game that prefered application of ANN - an AI algorithms and mix it with a random classic one. I and my partner decided to build gomoku game. After 3 fail times, we changed the decision to build snake game. then we get 9 from teacher, the highest score of that course. that was grate. I think the success come to us because of we understand each other and learn together before each other mistake without blaming."

### ✅ Cue Card Coverage

| Cue Card Point | Covered? | How Well |
|----------------|:--------:|----------|
| What the project was | ✅ | ANN + classic AI game — clear |
| Your role | ⚠️ | **Not mentioned** — examiner loses track of YOU |
| Who you worked with | ✅ | 2-member team, partner |
| How your team made decisions | ⚠️ | Partially (gomoku → snake switch), but no decision-making explanation |
| Whether successful | ✅ | 9.0, highest in class + why (understanding, no blame) |

### ✅ Strengths
- **Excellent narrative arc** — fail 3 times → pivot → succeed → highest score. This is a strong story.
- **Genuine reflection** — "learned from each other's mistakes without blaming" is a mature insight.

### 🔧 Corrections & Vocabulary Learned

| Original | Fixed |
|----------|-------|
| `At the time before` | **Back then** / **At that time** |
| `My teams with 2 members` | **My partner and I** |
| `received requirement to create` | **were given a project brief to create** |
| `prefered application of ANN` | **to demonstrate a practical application of an ANN** |
| `mix it with a random classic one` | **combined with a classical AI algorithm** |
| `I and my partner decided` | **My partner and I initially decided** |
| `After 3 fail times` | **After three failed attempts** |
| `then we get 9 from teacher` | **we scored 9 out of 10, the highest in the course** |
| `that was grate` | **that was a great achievement** |
| `learn together before each other mistake` | **learn from each other's mistakes** |
| `without blaming` | **without blaming one another** — ✅ strong & natural! |

### 💡 Fixed Answer (Band 7.0+)

> *"Let me talk about a project I worked on with my partner last year. We were given a project brief to create a game that demonstrated a practical application of an Artificial Neural Network combined with a classical AI algorithm.*
>
> *At first, my partner and I decided to build a Gomoku game using ANN-based pathfinding. Unfortunately, it didn't work out — we failed three times. After those attempts, we made the decision to switch to the Snake game instead, which turned out to be a much better fit for our time constraints.*
>
> *We ended up scoring 9 out of 10 — the highest mark in the entire course. That was a huge achievement for us. I believe the success came from the fact that we understood each other well and learned from each other's mistakes without blaming one another. We divided the tasks based on our strengths — I handled the AI model while she managed the documentation — and that made the collaboration very smooth."*

### 💡 Part 2 Structure Tips

```
Opening (10 sec)     → "Let me talk about..." — set the scene
Development (60 sec) → Walk through the story: challenge → decision → process
Conclusion (20 sec)  → Explain WHY it was successful + reflection
```

### ⚠️ Issues
- **Incomplete** — answer cuts off after 2 sentences.
- **Limited development** — Part 3 requires **opinion + reason + example + contrast**.

### 🔧 Corrections & Vocabulary Learned

| Original | Fixed |
|----------|-------|
| `the point said` | **the point that was made** |
| `difficulty of changing ability` | **the difficulty of adapting our skills** |
| `unstop wold` | **an ever-changing world** |
| `Expectialy` | **Especially** |
| `which one can be done with AI will be added to the others` | **tasks that can be done with AI will be added to our existing responsibilities** |
| `Each people` | **Each person** / **Everyone** |
| `have to do more things than the time before within AI support` | **has to handle more tasks than before, with the help of AI** |

### 💡 Fixed Version (Band 6.0+)
> *"I agree with the point that it is very difficult to adapt our skills in an ever-changing world, especially with the AI generation arriving. First, all careers are changing. Tasks that can be done with AI will be added to our existing responsibilities, meaning each person has to handle more tasks than before, with the support of AI. For example, in my field of AI engineering, I now need to understand not just coding, but also data pipelines, model deployment, and even business strategy — things that used to be handled by separate teams."*

### 💡 How to develop Part 3 answers (full example)

If you had continued, here's how a Band 7+ answer would look:

> *"I agree with the point that it is very difficult to adapt our skills in an ever-changing world, especially with the AI generation arriving. First, all careers are changing. Tasks that can be done with AI will be added to our existing responsibilities, meaning each person has to handle more tasks than before, with the support of AI.*
>
> *However, I also think this is an opportunity. People who are willing to learn and adapt will thrive, while those who resist change may fall behind. For example, in my field of AI engineering, I now need to understand not just coding, but also data pipelines, model deployment, and even business strategy — things that used to be handled by separate teams.*
>
> *So while the challenge is real, I believe the key is to embrace continuous learning rather than fear the change."*

## 🎓 IELTS Examiner Notes & Action Plan

### Part 1: What to fix first
1. **The 10-Second Proofread** — review each answer before sending; catches typos (`core` → `score`) and missing words (`and listen to`).
2. **Answer the question first** — in Part 1, always lead with a direct answer, then explain. Keep stories short; save detail for Part 2.
3. **Learn standard collocations**: `tight deadline`, `meet the deadline`, `work under pressure`, `prioritize tasks`, `huge workload`.

### Part 2: What to fix first
1. **Always open with "Let me talk about..."** — gives you 2 seconds to organize your thoughts.
2. **Mention your role** — even a short phrase: "As the AI lead, I was responsible for..."
3. **Use time-markers**: *Initially*, *After that*, *Eventually*, *In the end*.

### Part 3: What to fix first
1. **Structure: Opinion → Reason → Example → Contrast** — always develop at least 2 sentences.
2. **End with a balanced view** — "While X is true, I also think Y" shows maturity.
3. **Use the "For example" technique** — your own experience (AI engineering) is a perfect example.
