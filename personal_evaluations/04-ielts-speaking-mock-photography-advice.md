## 📝 PART 1 — Photography & Memories (Transcript tóm tắt + đánh giá)

### Q1: Do you enjoy taking photos? Why or why not?
> *"I enjoy taking photo, but uh I would like, uh I'm like to uh do that with my friend uh because I feel uh uh uncomfortable when I do that. Uh yeah, I just uh want to do that by myself."*

- ⚠️ **Mâu thuẫn (contradictory)**: "enjoy" vs "uncomfortable", "with friend" vs "by myself" → mất Coherence ngay.
- ✅ **Bản sửa (6.0):** *"Yes, I do enjoy taking photos, but only when I'm with my close friends. To be honest, I feel a bit uncomfortable taking pictures by myself, so I usually only take photos when I'm out with them."*

### Q2: Smartphone or digital camera?
> *"Um, I like to taking photo with uh um with phone because I don't have uh uh the the camera."*

- `I like to taking` → **I prefer taking** | `the camera` → **a camera**
- ✅ **Bản sửa:** *"I definitely prefer taking photos with my smartphone, because I don't own a digital camera. Besides, my phone is always with me, so it's much more convenient — I can capture a moment and share it instantly."*

### Q3: How do you store or organize your photos? *(xin repeat lần 1)*
> *"Um, yeah, I just um I just take a photo uh at uh like uh I I just take a random photo. I I don't organize it because I don't uh know how to do that."*

- `I don't organize it` → **`I don't organize them`** (photos số nhiều)
- ✅ **Bản sửa:** *"To be honest, I just take random photos and I don't organize them at all, because I don't really know how to. My photos just stay in my phone's camera roll, and occasionally I delete the blurry ones."*

### Q4: Too many photos instead of enjoying the moment? *(xin repeat lần 2)*
> *"Um, I think that is uh the way they uh enjoy uh their moment. Uh because uh take a photo like uh like uh uh saving the photo forever."*

- 💡 **Ý rất hay!** — "Chụp ảnh chính là cách họ tận hưởng khoảnh khắc, vì lưu giữ kỷ niệm mãi mãi."
- ✅ **Bản sửa:** *"I actually think taking photos **is** a way for people to enjoy the moment, because it's like **preserving** that memory **forever**. When you look back at the photo, you can relive the moment again."*

## 📝 PART 2 — Cue Card: Important Advice (Transcript tóm tắt + đánh giá)

> Nội dung câu chuyện: Old man ở chỗ làm → "a man before 40 never knows how his family loves him, too late when he understands" → về nhà xin lỗi → nhận ra gia đình thương mình → chăm sóc gia đình.

### ⭐ Điểm mạnh lớn nhất của toàn bài!
- ✅ Đủ 4 điểm cue card (ai cho, tình huống, lời khuyên, kết quả)
- ✅ Cảm xúc thật, cá nhân, đáng nhớ — có *quoted advice* ("a man before 40 never knows...") = kỹ thuật trích dẫn ăn điểm
- ✅ Kéo dài >2 phút, có cấu trúc
- **Phần ý đáng 7.0 — ngôn ngữ kéo xuống ~5.0**

### 🔧 Sửa lỗi chính (theo transcript)
| Transcript | Sửa |
|---|---|
| `the old man uh in my workspace uh at at the time before` | **an old man I worked with, back then** ⚠️ *("at the time before" lặp lại từ mock trước)* |
| `when I very uncomfortable and to my family complain` | **when I was very upset because my family kept complaining** |
| `he tell me` | **he told me** ❗ (past tense) |
| `because of that advice I come back` | **thanks to that advice, I decided to come back home** |
| `apologize my family` | **apologize TO my family** ❗ |
| `cross over the that situation` | **get through that situation** |
| `take care my family` | **take care OF my family** ❗ |
| `fix my bad thing day-to-day` | **improve my bad habits day by day** |

### ✅ Bản mẫu (Band 6.5–7.0)
> *"I'd like to talk about a piece of advice that completely changed my life. It was given by an old man I worked with back then.*
>
> *At the time, I had a huge fight with my family — they kept complaining about me, and I was so upset that I decided to leave home forever. When the old man saw how frustrated I was, he told me something I've never forgotten: 'A man before the age of 40 never truly knows how much his family loves him — and by the time he realizes it, it's often too late.'*
>
> *That advice really shook me. I went home, apologized to my family, and we managed to get through that difficult situation. Looking back, it helped me recognize how much my parents and my sister love me, and now I always remind myself to take care of them and improve my bad habits day by day, based on their feedback.*
>
> *So, in the end, that short conversation with a stranger turned out to be one of the most valuable lessons of my life."*

## 📝 PART 3 — Seeking & Giving Advice (Transcript + đánh giá)

### Q1: Parents or friends?
> *"I think the young people don't usually turn to... especially from their parents, because they think the lesson from parents that they know before. And they don't want to turn to... from friends, because they don't want to be ditch by someone like them."*

- ⚠️ Ý khó hiểu, thiếu kết luận (ai là người họ thực sự tìm đến)
- ✅ **Bản sửa:** *"I think young people nowadays are less likely to turn to their parents, because they believe they already know those lessons — parents' advice sounds old-fashioned to them. On the other hand, many of them also avoid asking friends, because they're afraid of being judged. So they often end up searching for advice on the internet or keeping their problems to themselves."*

### Q2: When can peer/social media advice be unreliable? 💥
- **⚠️ KHÔNG được trả lời** — transcript nhảy thẳng sang "the most effective way to advice..." → mất điểm Task Response.
- ✅ **Bản mẫu:** *"Peer and social media advice can be very unreliable, because it's usually based on personal experience rather than expertise. For example, a friend might tell you to invest in a certain stock because it worked for them, but they have no knowledge of financial risk. Similarly, on social media, people often give advice without knowing your full situation, so following it blindly can actually make things worse. That's why we should always verify advice from multiple reliable sources before acting on it."*

### Q3: Should leaders always listen to their team? *(câu trả lời tốt nhất Part 3)*
> *"more people create more insight... but they have to keep their things clearly"*

- ✅ Cấu trúc rõ: Đồng ý → lý do → caveat
- Sửa: `listen their teams suggestion` → **listen TO their team's suggestions**; `it help` → **it helps**; `keep their things clearly` → **make the final decision clear**
- ✅ **Bản sửa:** *"Yes, I believe leaders and managers should listen to their team's suggestions, because more people mean more insights — it creates more opportunities to overcome any issue. However, listening doesn't mean they must follow everything. In the end, the leader needs to make the final decision clearly and take responsibility for it, and then the whole team should commit to that decision."*

### Q4: How has seeking advice changed with digital tools & AI? *(câu lửng)*
> *"the people can fight out... the people have to keep calm before they active."*

- ⚠️ Chưa hoàn chỉnh & khó hiểu
- ✅ **Bản mẫu (Band 6.5+):** *"I think the way people seek advice has changed dramatically. In the past, you had to ask someone you knew, or pay a professional for guidance. Nowadays, with digital tools and AI, people can get instant answers — they simply ask an AI chatbot for career or health advice at any time of day, without spending any money.*
  *However, there's a trade-off. AI advice is often too generic, because it doesn't truly understand your personal situation. So while technology has made advice more accessible and affordable, I believe people still need to think critically and verify the information before acting on it.*
  *In short, technology has changed the 'how', but the quality of advice still depends on human judgment."*
