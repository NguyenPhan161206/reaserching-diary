### Questions 1–5

**Jensen Huang — Background**

Jensen Huang is the CEO of __________ (1), a company that became one of the most valuable in the world.

The company led a fundamental shift in how computers work by introducing __________ (2) processing.

In the early 1990s, Huang observed that only about __________ (3) percent of code in a software program does 99% of the processing.

The __________ (4) percent of the code must be done sequentially.

Huang chose video games as the first market because he believed it had the potential to become the largest market for __________ (5) ever.

---

### ✅ Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | **NVIDIA** | Jensen Huang is the CEO of NVIDIA — stated in the intro |
| 2 | **parallel** | "He's the CEO of NVIDIA... they led a fundamental shift... parallel processing" |
| 3 | **10** / **ten** | "There are just a few lines of code — maybe 10% of the code — does 99% of the processing" |
| 4 | **90** / **ninety** | "The other 90% of the code has to be done sequentially" |
| 5 | **entertainment** | "Video games has potential to be the largest market for entertainment ever" |

## PART 2 — Multiple Choice (Easy–Medium)

> **Skill tested:** Understanding main ideas and specific details
> **Instructions:** Choose the correct letter: A, B, or C.
> **Source chapters:** 7:59–15:40

### Questions 6–10

**6. What is CUDA?**

A. A new type of GPU chip
B. A platform that lets programmers use GPUs with familiar programming languages
C. A software for training neural networks

**7. Why did creating CUDA matter?**

A. It made GPUs cheaper for consumers
B. It gave more people easier access to parallel computing power
C. It replaced the need for CPUs entirely

**8. What happened in 2012 that changed everything?**

A. NVIDIA released a new graphics card
B. A team of researchers trained a neural network called AlexNet on NVIDIA GPUs and won an image recognition competition
C. CUDA was officially launched for the first time

**9. What was the "big breakthrough" Jensen saw after AlexNet?**

A. That GPUs could replace CPUs for all tasks
B. That deep learning could potentially reshape the entire computer industry
C. That video games would become the main application for GPUs

**10. How long did it take from AlexNet (2012) to the current AI boom?**

A. About 3 years
B. About 5 years
C. About 8–10 years

---

### ✅ Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 6 | **B** | "CUDA... lets programmers tell the GPU what to do using programming languages that they already know, like C" |
| 7 | **B** | "It gives way more people easier access to all of this computing power" |
| 8 | **B** | "In 2012, a group of three researchers submits an entry... called AlexNet... they used Nvidia GPUs" |
| 9 | **B** | "We rightfully reasoned that... it has the potential of reshaping the computer industry altogether" |
| 10 | **C** | "Eight years. Eight, 10 years." (Huang's direct answer) |
